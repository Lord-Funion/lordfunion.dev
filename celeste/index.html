One level format

Build anywhere. Play on the calculator.

Studio and CELEDIT both export CELV levels as calculator AppVars. Transfer the resulting .8xv, open CEleste’s custom-level browser with MODE, and play. Finishing the last room now lands on a summit-style results screen with your time and deaths, plus replay, level-select, and title choices.

From download to dash

Install in three steps

You need a TI-84 Plus CE or TI-83 Premium CE, a USB cable, and the CE C libraries.

  1. 1

    Get the programs

    Download CELESTE.8xp. Add CELEDIT.8xp if you want to build on-calc, and install the C Libraries.

  2. 2

    Send them to the calculator

    Use TI Connect CE or TiLP to transfer the program files and any custom `.8xv` levels.

  3. 3

    Climb

    Run prgmCELESTE. Press MODE on the title screen for custom levels, then 2ND to launch the selected level.

If your OS blocks assembly programs, follow the arTIfiCE instructions for your calculator first.
